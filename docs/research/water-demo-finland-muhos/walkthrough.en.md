# Muhos: what happens to water when forest land becomes a data-centre site?

Real project setting and dated official evidence; an educational reconstruction, not a finding of criminal guilt or a prediction of completed construction.

## Default view and actions

Open at M0 with a schematic forest/peatland setting, project boundary and nearby Oulujoki. Show a short change animation, one water card and one wider-system connection at each step. The optional rainfall replay starts off. Persistent labels distinguish reported events, planning estimates, teaching examples and unknowns.

Continue, Back, direct M0–M6 timeline selection, orbit/zoom/reset camera, replay/skip animation, optional rainfall and evidence details. No mandatory task, quiz or new planning editor. Restore complete states on jumps; never accumulate impacts on replay. At M6, the older TVE1 calculation is optional historical planning detail; it never becomes an active operating forecast.

At M2 show the current work-pause status. Continuing to M3–M6 opens a clearly labelled conditional future, not a claim that permits exist or work may resume. Essential protective/monitoring work during the pause is distinct from further development.

Rainfall arrows are qualitative route illustrations. Gross rain volume is not measured runoff, treatment overflow or water entering the river. No rain replay at M1; no winter simulation. Do not animate the whole river changing colour or fish dying.

**Status:** authority_documented_current_context

## Optional questions

**Did Google have no permissions at all?**

The documented dispute concerns EIA sequencing and, at Muhos, a water-permit requirement for drainage. Some notifications and municipal processes existed. The October authority record and police referral do not establish a criminal conviction.

**Will homes lose water?**

No household shortage is established. The 2025 river-water option describes a dedicated river intake because local waterworks and aquifer capacity are insufficient for its cooling requirement. This is not evidence of an existing residential supply cut.

**Will air cooling remove the construction impacts?**

Changing heat rejection does not reverse forest removal, ditching or exposed-soil runoff. The EIA air-cooling alternative needs its own confirmed operational water budget; it must not inherit the water-cooling upper estimate.

**Can we stop monitoring when development pauses?**

A pause does not automatically stop rainfall, drainage or oxidation of disturbed material. Necessary protective work and monitoring have a different purpose from continued construction.
