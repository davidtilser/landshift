# What happened at Google's Finnish data-centre sites?

**Evidence cutoff: 24 October 2026.** The current controversy concerns preparatory forest clearance and earthworks at **Muhos and Kajaani-Otanmäki**. This packet selects **Muhos**, because official documents provide a specific river/drainage/acidification story. The statement “Google cleared forest without any permission” compresses several different issues and should not be the demo's legal conclusion.

## Why “no permit” needs qualification

EIA sequencing, forest-use declarations, landscape/building permissions and drainage/water-law authorization are different questions. Kajaani's municipal clarification describes forest-use declarations and its position that no landscape-work permit was required at logging time. It does not settle the EIA dispute or determine Muhos's permissions. [City of Kajaani clarification](https://kdce.fi/city-of-kajaani-clarifies-the-progress-of-googles-data-center-project-in-kajaani/)

For Muhos drainage specifically, the detailed October authority document concludes that the notified measures require a Water Act permit. That official position is more precise for this water demo than a blanket assertion that every activity lacked all permissions. The packet does not independently adjudicate Finnish law. [LVV response, printed pp.3–6](https://www.sttinfo.fi/files/69821631/72435808/532934/fi)

There is also a **different Hamina incident**: Yle reports roughly 1.3 ha of flying-squirrel habitat cleared without permission during expansion in 2018–2019. Do not merge that area, date or permitting issue with Muhos's 2026 clearance. Likewise, do not transfer Hamina's operating seawater-cooling system to inland Muhos. [Yle, 20 October](https://yle.fi/a/74-20252425)

## Why Muhos is a useful water case

The strongest site-specific extension beyond SF is **disturbed land → ditches → water protection → Oulujoki**, with a chemistry mechanism as well as a volume question. The attached authority document identifies drainage scopes and sulfide-bearing material; exact numerical water quality is not available here. It also distinguishes concerns close to outfalls from smaller effects after mixing at whole-river scale. The demo must not turn that into uniform river-wide pollution, fish mortality or a predicted household impact. [LVV response](https://www.sttinfo.fi/files/69821631/72435808/532934/fi)

Its water-cooling alternative would add a separate river-intake/return pathway, but **it must not be presented as the selected current design**. The primary 2025 programme evaluates two technologies. Kaleva reports intended air cooling for Muhos and the other new sites; no final primary design/water-budget confirmation was obtained. That reporting is material evidence against defaulting to the programme's old maximum water figure. [Kaleva](https://www.kaleva.fi/googlen-datakeskukset-on-tarkoitus-jaahdyttaa-ilma/13668890)

## Material findings for the model

- Use real project and drainage reference areas without pretending they are the same footprint.
- Use the October pause as current context. Later construction/opening/operation is an explicitly conditional preview.
- Show the water-quality mechanism qualitatively. Do not invent pH, metal loads or control efficiency.
- Use verified offsite FMI weather for a labelled rainfall illustration. Gross precipitation is computable; site runoff is not yet calibrated.
- Keep air-cooling intent, older river-cooling estimates and actual operating demand distinct.
- Do not infer wolves entering villages, ecological deaths or household shortages from clearance area alone. These are outside this water-only model and were not established by this investigation.

The SF-style story structure is reusable. Its soil, rainfall, cooling intensity, sewer route and utility assumptions are not Finnish inputs. Exact sources, pages and fingerprints are in [sources.json](sources.json); the implementation contract is in [README.md](README.md).
