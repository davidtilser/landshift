Summer Reliability

Recent summer events have demonstrated how the increased frequency of climate-driven extreme heat, drought, and wildfires have strained California’s electricity supply and demand. The CEC conducts analyses to estimate and prepare for gaps between supply and demand during average and extreme weather events.

When temperatures rise in summer, so does demand for electricity across the state, particularly in the form of increased air conditioning use. Heightened demand can also coincide with reduced energy imports due to neighboring states facing similar heat patterns, and reduced in-state hydroelectric power production due to drought. Wildfires can also disrupt power generation and transmission lines, further impacting supply. To keep Californians safe and ensure a successful transition to a reliable, affordable, clean energy future, the state is working to rapidly deploy new projects and maintain existing resources for emergency use. Consumers are also encouraged to participate in conserving electricity when possible as outlined below.

Now Available

The California Energy Resource and Reliability Outlook, 2026, released in July 2026, provides a comprehensive, statewide assessment of electric and gas energy resource planning and reliability for the period of 2026-2030, for all investor-owned utilities and publicly owned utilities in California.

Ways to Help

The public, consumers and businesses can do their part to help when California experiences extreme weather that causes tight energy supplies. Here are some ways to help:

  • Voluntarily conserve energy during a Flex Alert
  • Get paid to conserve energy during grid emergencies through new and innovative demand response programs:
    • Residential
    • Large electric customers and third-party aggregators

Summer Reliability Analysis

Summer 2026
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment: SB 846 Third Quarterly Report 2026
    Provides the 2026 third quarterly review of the electricity supply forecast and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory and includes an updated analysis for summer 2026.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment: SB 846 Second Quarterly Report 2026
    Provides the 2026 second quarterly review of the supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory and includes an updated analysis for summer 2026.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment: Covering the Requirements of SB 846 (First Quarterly Report for 2026) and SB 1020 (Annual Report)
    Provides the first quarterly review of 2026, including the demand forecast, supply forecast, and potential high, medium, and low risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory from 2026 to 2036, as required by SB 846. As required by SB 1020, this report also provides a joint reliability progress report that reviews system and local reliability, with a particular focus on summer reliability.
Summer 2025
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - SB 846 Fourth Quarterly Report 2025
    Provides the 2025 fourth quarterly review of the electricity supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory and includes an updated analysis for summer 2025.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment: SB 846 Third Quarterly Report 2025
    Provides the 2025 third quarterly review of the electricity supply forecast and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory, and includes an updated analysis for summer 2025.
     
  • California Energy Resource and Reliability Outlook, 2025 - provides a comprehensive, statewide assessment of electric and gas energy resource planning and reliability for the period of 2025-2029, for all investor-owned utilities and publicly owned utilities in California.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment: Covering the Requirements of SB 846 (Combined First and Second Quarterly Report for 2025) and SB 1020 (Annual Report) - Provides the first quarterly review of the 2025 demand forecast, supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory from 2025 to 2035, as required by Senate Bill 846. Includes an updated analysis for summer 2025. This report also provides a joint reliability progress report, as required by Senate Bill 1020, that reviews system and local reliability, with a particular focus on summer reliability.
Summer 2024
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - SB 846 Fourth Quarterly Report 2024 - Provides the 2024 fourth quarterly review of the supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory and includes an updated analysis for summer 2024.
     
  • California Energy Resource and Reliability Outlook 2024 - provides a comprehensive, statewide assessment of electric and natural gas energy resource planning and reliability for the  period of 2024-2028, for all investor-owned utilities and publicly owned utilities in California.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - SB 846 Combined Second and Third Quarterly Report 2024 - Provides the 2024 combined second and third quarterly review of the supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory for 2024 and includes an updated analysis for summer 2024.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment: Covering the Requirements of SB 846 (First Quarterly Report for 2024) and SB 1020 (Annual Report) - Provides the first quarterly review of the 2024 demand forecast, supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory from 2024 to 2032, as required by Senate Bill 846 (Dodd, Chapter 239, Statutes of 2022). Includes an updated analysis for summer 2024. This report also provides a joint reliability progress report, as required by Senate Bill 1020, that reviews system and local reliability, with a particular focus on summer reliability.
Summer 2023
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - Fourth Quarterly Report - Provides the fourth quarterly review of the demand forecast, supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory from 2023 to 2032, as required by Senate Bill 846 (Dodd, Chapter 239, Statutes of 2022). Includes an updated analysis for summer 2023.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - Third Quarterly Report - Provides the third quarterly review of the demand forecast, supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory from 2023 to 2032, as required by Senate Bill 846 (Dodd, Chapter 239, Statutes of 2022). Includes an updated analysis for summer 2023.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - Second Quarterly Report - Provides the second quarterly review of the demand forecast, supply forecast, and risks to reliability in the California Independent System Operator territory from 2023 to 2032, as required by Senate Bill 846 (Dodd, Chapter 239, Statutes of 2022). Includes an updated analysis for summer 2023.
     
  • Joint Agency Reliability Planning Assessment - Addresses requirements for electricity reliability reporting in Senate Bill 846 (Dodd, Chapter 239, Statutes of 2022) and Assembly Bill 205 (Committee on Budget, Chapter 61, Statutes of 2022).
     
  • Clean Energy Reliability Investment Plan - Addresses a requirement in Senate Bill 846 (Dodd, Chapter 239, Statutes of 2022) for the California Energy Commission to develop a $1 billion investment plan for clean energy resources.
Summer 2022
  • 2022 Summer Stack Analysis - Examines the potential impacts of a west-wide extreme weather event and prolonged drought in the critical months of July though September between 2022 and 2026.
Summer 2021
  • Emergency Proclamation - Report responding to the Governor's July 30, 2021 Emergency Proclamation.
     
  • 2021 Summer Stack Analysis - Estimates the potential gap between supply and demand under average and extreme weather conditions similar to those in summer 2020 to plan for additional contingencies.
     
  • Summer 2021 and 2022 Reliability Analysis - Review of Summer 2020 outages, 2021 heat events, and how grid reliability may be impacted in the Summer 2022.
     
  • Midterm Reliability Analysis - Provides the results of analyses conducted by CEC staff to inform decisions about the need for future resource procurement to support reliability for the midterm (2023 – 2026).
     
  • 2021 Joint Agencies CAISO Balancing Authority Area Electric Reliability Contingency Plan - Describes how the CEC, CPUC, and CAISO will coordinate in advance of and throughout an anticipated electricity supply shortfall event in the CAISO balancing area, describes contingency resources that can support the grid during an anticipated shortfall, and describes the roles and responsibilities of each energy institution to identify and pursue contingency resources, as well as the triggers associated with engaging each resource.
Summer 2020
  • Joint Agency Root Cause Analysis - Findings which are attributed to the mid-August 2020 rotating electricity outages amongst a West-wide extreme heat wave.
