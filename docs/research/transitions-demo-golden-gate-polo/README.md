# Golden Gate Park: transitions across the demo

This is the shared **English transition direction** for the fictional SF Polo Fields demo: water, energy, land/space and the three selected proposals. It joins the existing packets into visual stories that develop across milestones. It supplies a content/data handoff, not game-engine implementation or new environmental calculations. San José and Muhos remain separate research cases; no SF surface, weather or transition output is transferred to them.

## The task and intended experience

Find what starts, builds up, stays constant, switches pathways, recedes, recovers or repeats. For each story, identify its beginning and ending states, visible mechanism, learner takeaway and evidence boundary. A milestone card should be a pause in a continuing story, rather than an unrelated static scene.

Use the **five-chapter timeline Today → Foundations → Structure → Ready → Operating** (stable runtime IDs M0, M1, M4, M5, M6). The original seven analytical snapshots remain the evidence layer. Preparation is compressed inside Foundations; frame/roof rise in Structure; Ready remains distinct from Operating. See the [runtime binding](../../polo-demo.md#five-chapter-contract-and-retained-use-cases). Runtime M1 is a new foundation checkpoint with no standalone resource budget or runoff estimate; research M1 below still means stripping-only. M2/M3 are research comparisons rather than extra visible stops. Preserve visible anchors: campus boundary, original turf, access corridor and schematic service strip. On Continue, animate the relevant change and settle into the selected milestone's complete state. The user may skip, replay, jump or go back. Do not require an interaction to advance.

**Earlier-state navigation is a comparison, not physical reversal.** Going from M4 to M1 restores the earlier snapshot with a comparison label; it does not imply that a building can vanish, soil recover instantly or pollution reverse. M1→M4 and M4→M6 can be forward story spans even when effects move in opposite directions: construction pressure grows, then temporary pressure recedes while permanent occupation remains.

## Analytical story spans and their five-chapter binding

The M0–M6 labels below refer to preserved research snapshots. Apply them through the five-chapter binding above: M1–M3 preparation plays within Foundations, original M4 construction spans Foundations/Structure, M5 maps to Ready and M6 maps to Operating. Foundation-specific quantities are not inferred from the original stage inventories.

### M0→M1: begin the intervention

Keep the original boundary visible as turf is removed. Before heavy access begins, show the labelled illustrative barriers/diversion on the separate access corridor. Reveal work-water and fuel symbols alongside the selected work activity. Keep existing users visible and neutral. Do not start campus operating flows or add a quantified M1 rain result.

### M1→M4: build pressure without counting land twice

Use persistent ground textures and the same vehicle entry direction to connect stripping, grading, compaction and construction. The worked campus area stays 3.2 ha even as the type of work changes. Keep external access wear qualitatively visible through construction; its area is not included in the campus total. Machinery changes with each selected inventory rather than accumulating as a permanent fleet.

Water and fuel cards resolve to the selected step totals. Do not animate a smoothly rising demand curve from totals covering different stage lengths. A visual transition can morph geometry or fade activity; numerical cards update to supported endpoints with their time basis. During a rainfall comparison, M1 is **Not quantified** and M2→M3 is a plateau, not an extra runoff penalty.

### M4→M5: finish works and reveal the whole footprint

Complete the authored surface allocation, remove the construction machines and temporary work symbols, and present the commissioning inventory separately. The temporary access overlay starts repair and reopening as an explicitly authored handover scene. No actual park route or service approval is implied.

Play the [land reveal](../land-space-demo-golden-gate-polo/README.md): 1.2 ha building outline → supporting surfaces → full 4 ha campus. Ghost original turf and schematic sports markings through the completed site. Retained original turf and new planting remain distinct. A recurring community question can change from **“How do I get through during works?”** to **“Can this place still serve the original use?”**

The roof tank becomes available. It starts empty only when the user opens an independent event replay; do not show it permanently full or count captured water as supplied operating water.

### M5→M6: temporary pressure recedes, recurring demand begins

Keep the finished surface allocation unchanged. End commissioning visuals and start the separate annual-average operating view: electricity splits into IT and supporting systems, while cooling water splits into supply, evaporation and blowdown. Commissioning fills do not recur with the operating loop. Annual display values are not added again on replay, and average flows are not peak demands.

The access-repair illustration may show grass becoming greener over an uncalibrated, labelled time-compressed replay. Do not promise a recovery date or show full soil recovery. The campus remains clearly visible through that recovery: repair of temporary external damage does not undo permanent land occupation or offset its sealed area.

### Inside M6: proposals have their own lifecycle

Keep the original M6 state saved. Each prepared proposal begins from that original state, introduces its intervention, shows the changed mechanism and offers return/reset. The phases are **Original / Proposed works or layers / Active concept / Optional limitation replay**, not new milestone IDs. A proposal installation may be replayed after entering M6 without claiming a retrofit duration or that it was already installed during baseline construction.

For heat, keep residual cooling and recipient backup heating available; separate circulation from heat transfer across isolated circuits. The recipient-unavailable replay changes acceptance of heat rather than stopping computing. The completed teaching case selects a heat pump under explicit temperature assumptions. Heat results are **Calculated teaching data — UI pending**; the [packet and future display recommendation](../heat-demo-golden-gate-polo/README.md) are complete, while the scene is not updated by this data task.

For recycled water, switch only the highlighted source route; a dissolve or explanatory pulse is not a measured network switchover. For the green roof, rain and dry-condition beats are illustrative conditions, not a simulated weather year. Switching either concept on removes **all numerical overlays**, including baseline water, energy and land statistics and any previous heat results. Switching cases clears prior equipment, plants, pipes, temporary works and result state; proposals do not stack.

## Time, evidence and rendering rules

- Distinguish **milestone progression**, **independent rain event**, **average operating day/year**, **proposal phases** and **illustrative recovery/dry conditions**. Show the active mode's time/evidence label. Animation seconds are never workdays, storm minutes or recovery months.
- Animate the causal mechanism; update numeric cards from endpoint values. Do not interpolate nulls, guess a monthly trajectory, invent a demand curve or scale pipe width, rainfall particles or recovery colour as a measurement.
- Compaction can alter physical condition without a different surface-budget or runoff-model category. Preserve the plateau rather than manufacture a new number to justify animation.
- Tank fill may use the verified event-storage endpoint; intermediate frames are illustrative, not a routed hydrograph. Reset it to the selected empty-start assumption for each comparison.
- Temporary repair and planted roofs do not cancel original land-use loss, prove soil restoration or establish public access. Neighbour benefits and extra infrastructure can coexist.
- Keep homes, reservoirs and generic drainage endpoints neutral. No invented blackouts, empty taps, polluted lakes, sewer overload or new treatment/substation capacity.
- Restore a complete target state on jumps. Reverse navigation never runs fluid flows backwards; proposal unavailability is a separate defined mechanism, not time reversal.
